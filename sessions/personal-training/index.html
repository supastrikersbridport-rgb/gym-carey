Old-school, and proud of it

In true old-school style, you'll track your lifts with a notepad and pencil, so you can see real progress over time.

Whether you're rehabbing an injury, aiming for weight loss or just want to enjoy lifting in a supportive environment, Nigel will help you get there. Biomechanics is practically a hobby: he's been studying it for over 20 years.

Come back stronger from injury

Nigel specialises in helping people train around injuries. From knee replacements to torn biceps, he's been there himself and knows how to help you come back stronger.
