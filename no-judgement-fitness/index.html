Seminars and courses

As part of building a community around the gym, we host seminars and courses through the year. Topics might include the best way to squat for your body type, or how to train around and rehabilitate elbow pain. We're always open to ideas.

They're open to the public, and members get first dibs and a discount on seats.

Monthly member challenges

Nothing motivates like a bit of healthy competition. Beating your own personal best is one thing, but chasing someone else's lights a fire under you.

We run a different member challenge regularly, so there's always something to aim for.
