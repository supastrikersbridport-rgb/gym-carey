Why join Gym Carey?

There's no shortage of gyms, from independents like us to shiny commercial chains. We like to think we do things a little differently.

A welcoming environment

Some people try a gym once, find bellowing machismo and glaring strangers, and never go back. That's not us. Everyone leaves their ego at the door, looks out for each other and sticks to gentle motivation.

We help you find your pace

There's no one-size-fits-all approach. We've seen great results from two sessions a week and from four hard ones. We'll help you work out what exercises, frequency and intensity suit you.

Anybody is welcome

Being beginner-friendly doesn't mean we turn experienced lifters away. As long as you bring a positive attitude and a willingness to help others, there's a place for you here.

A proper induction to get you started

Our full one-to-one induction takes around 90 minutes. You'll leave with everything written in your own book, so you can come along and train with confidence. Support is always on offer after that too.
