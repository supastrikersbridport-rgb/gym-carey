Meet Nigel and Roxanne

We've both been where you are. That's why we keep Gym Carey friendly, patient and free of egos.

N

Nigel Carey

Owner and personal trainer

Nigel has more than 30 years in the gym, including competitive powerlifting and strongman. He's also had a torn bicep and a full knee replacement, so he knows what it takes to come back from injury.

R

Roxanne

Coach

Roxanne started with HIIT classes around ten years ago and hasn't looked back. She loves helping people who feel intimidated by gyms find their confidence.

Knee rehab with someone who's been there

After his own knee replacement, Nigel now runs knee rehab sessions. Referrals are welcome.

Talk to Nigel

More than a gym

We run workshops, guest speakers and friendly challenges through the year. Our ego-free fundraising day, with a tombola, a BBQ and a tyre-pull competition, raised several hundred pounds for Weldmar Air Ambulance and Fightback Mental Health.

With thanks to Washingpool Farm, Lidl, Morrisons and Co-op Bridport, and Framptons Butchers for their support.
