Meet the team

Meet Nigel

Owner and personal trainer

With over 30 years of experience, Nigel Carey has lived and breathed every aspect of training, from competitive powerlifting and strongman to all-round fitness.

His philosophy is simple: lead from the front. When you train with him, he's not just counting reps. He's beside you, pushing through the work, proving that progress comes from effort, consistency and resilience.

Life has tested him with a few setbacks: a torn bicep and, most recently, a full knee replacement. Every time, Nigel has refused to quit.

Train with Nigel

Meet Roxanne

Nigel's wife and partner in the gym

Despite her husband's best efforts over the years, Roxanne has never been as big on the gym.

She found her start with HIIT classes around ten years ago, as a way of meeting new mums and making friends, and quickly learned to enjoy her new-found strength.

Roxanne knows how off-putting gyms can feel, but also how transformative exercise can be. She's reaped the physical and mental benefits of training around a busy schedule: proof you don't need to rewrite your timetable to suit the gym.

Knee rehab

Knee rehab has become a big part of Nigel's day, with rehab referrals coming his way. Who better to help than someone who's been through it?

The Gym Carey mission

To build a community as well as a thriving gym: a place where everyone is welcome, regardless of age or experience, whether you want to get stronger or lose body fat, and whether you're a dedicated athlete or a casual gym-goer.

We host seminars, guest speakers and challenges all year round to get everyone involved, motivated and moving towards their goals.
